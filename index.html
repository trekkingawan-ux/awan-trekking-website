We Are Your Kintamani Native Guides

Awan Trekking is a registered collective of professional native mountain guides located directly at the foot of Mount Batur, Kintamani, Bali. For over a decade, we have been leading travelers safely through volcanic ridges, black lava fields, and hidden waterfalls.

Unlike anonymous tour brokers, we directly organize every aspect of your journey. We prioritize small private groups, safe equipment, fair wages for local drivers, and preserving the sacred environment of our volcanic home.
